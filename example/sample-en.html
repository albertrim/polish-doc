1Where the days actually go

Support has been telling customers the bank is slow. That is not where most of the wait comes from. Two of the 3.4 days are ours.

❌ What we have been saying

"Refunds take three to five days because the bank has to process them."

✅ What is true

The bank takes one to three days. We add two before they ever see it.

I wrote the original support macro, and it put all of the delay on the bank. That one is on me. The macro gets rewritten before October 1.
